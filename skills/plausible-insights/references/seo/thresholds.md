# SEO interpretation

These are investigation heuristics, not validated universal quality scores.
Compare the same page intent, source mix, device mix, tracking configuration,
and complete periods. Show sample size and absolute counts beside percentages.

A relative traffic change of 15% or 30% can prioritize a check when the sample
is meaningful, but cannot establish a cause. A change from 2 to 1 visitor is
50% and not automatically an incident. Consider seasonality and incomplete days.

Bounce rate depends on pageviews and configured engagement events.
Single-page intent can be satisfied without another pageview.
Visit duration and time on page are different metrics with different
measurement limitations. Check the current
[Plausible definitions](https://plausible.io/docs/metrics-definitions)
before interpreting them. Neither is a direct reading-comprehension score.

High bounce, short time, or a traffic drop suggests questions, not diagnoses:
intent mismatch, routing/tracking changes, source mix, page performance,
seasonality, or successful single-page consumption are competing hypotheses.

Inspect real pages and, when relevant, routing and event tracking.
Use conversion goals, scroll depth and the site's own comparable baseline.
Do not claim a broken UX, ranking loss or algorithm penalty from analytics alone.
Do not stop campaigns, publish content, or change site configuration without
authorization for that action.
